  • morning bites (the early bird):
    breakfast & brunch spots worth waking up for

    where hungry people become happy people

    must i say more?

    bars, dives & places you'll remember (maybe)
morning bites
  • Veselka - 144 2nd Ave
    24/7 Ukrainian institution serving pierogi, borscht, and breakfast platters since 1954
  • Russ & Daughters Cafe - 127 Orchard St
    Best bagels & lox in the city, smoked fish platters
  • Banter - 132 Havemeyer St
    Australian-style brunch with avocado toast and coffee
  • Black Seed Bagels - 176 1st Ave
    Montreal-meets-NYC style bagels, wood-fired and hand-rolled
  • Superiority Burger - 119 Avenue A
    Vegetarian breakfast sandwiches and tofu scrambles
in-between bites
  • Artichoke Basille's Pizza - 328 E 14th St
    Famous for their indulgent artichoke slice - late-night legend
  • Mamoun's Falafel - 30 St Marks Pl
    Delicious Middle Eastern counter service since 1971
  • Superiority Burger - 119 Avenue A
    Cult vegetarian spot with creative veggie burgers and sides
  • Joe's Pizza - Multiple locations
    Classic NY slice - simple, perfect.
  • Vanessa's Dumpling House - 118 Eldridge St
    $1 dumplings and sesame pancakes
hearty plates
  • Prune - 54 E 1st St
    Intimate neighborhood spot with inventive seasonal American cooking
  • Contra - 138 Orchard St
    Minimalist tasting menu restaurant, Michelin-starred, creative and ambitious
  • Lil' Frankie's - 19 1st Ave
    Beloved Italian spot with wood-fired pizza and pasta, neighborhood vibe
  • Kafana - 116 Ave C
    Serbian restaurant with hearty Eastern European fare and cozy atmosphere
  • Ivan Ramen - 25 Clinton St
    NYC outpost of Tokyo ramen legend, rich bowls and innovative takes
sweet treats
  • Veniero's Pasticceria - 342 E 11th St
    Italian bakery since 1894 - legendary cannoli, tiramisu, and pastries
  • Morgenstern's Finest Ice Cream - 88 W Houston St
    Small-batch, inventive flavors, serious ice cream craftsmanship
  • Doughnut Plant - 379 Grand St
    Gourmet doughnuts, the tres leches is iconic
  • Mah-Ze-Dahr Bakery - Multiple locations
    Known for their brioche doughnuts and brown butter chocolate chip cookies
  • Supermoon Bakehouse - 120 Rivington St
    Instagram-famous croissant-donut hybrids with unique flavors
liquid courage
  • Death & Co - 433 E 6th St
    World-famous cocktail bar, dark and moody, exceptional drinks
  • Lovers Rock - 419 Tompkins Ave (Bed-Stuy, but worth it)
    Caribbean vibes, rum-forward cocktails, dancing
  • Standings - 43 E 7th St
    Sports bar with craft beer, burgers, and neighborhood energy
  • The Wayland - 700 E 9th St
    Whiskey-focused bar with Southern-inspired food and good vibes
  • Manitoba's - 99 Ave B
    Legendary dive bar owned by Dictators' Handsome Dick Manitoba, punk rock history
