Top Rated Restaurants

Experience the best of East Village dining. From iconic establishments to Instagram-worthy dishes, these are the spots you can't miss on your culinary tour.

Featured in NY Times

Veselka

★★★★★ (4.5/5)

Iconic 24-hour Ukrainian diner serving pierogi, borscht, and Eastern European comfort food since 1954. A true East Village institution.

144 2nd Avenue
$$ • Open 24 Hours
Most Photographed: Pierogies
Don't Miss: The potato and cheese pierogies with sour cream
honestly, veselka at 3am after a night out is THE move. get the challah french toast if you're there for breakfast, but real talk the pierogies are overrated by tourists.
insider tip: tuesday nights are weirdly empty. also the back room is way better than sitting by the window.
Critically Acclaimed

Rosella

★★★★★ (4.8/5)

A modern sushi restaurant redefining sustainability with locally sourced fish and creative omakase experiences. The space is warm, minimal, and distinctly East Village.

137 Avenue A
$$$ • Evening hours
Most Photographed: Hand rolls & amberjack sashimi
Don't Miss: The smoked trout hand roll with pickled daikon
rosella proves you don't need imported tuna to make great sushi. everything's local, seasonal, and surprisingly fresh. the chefs are chatty if you sit at the counter — they'll walk you through every bite.
insider tip: book the omakase early — it's one of the most thoughtful (and relaxed) sushi experiences downtown. pair it with their natural sake list.
Instagram Favorite

Vanessa's Dumpling House

★★★★☆ (4.3/5)

Casual Chinese eatery famous for hand-pulled noodles and incredibly affordable dumplings. Perfect for a quick, delicious bite.

118 Eldridge Street
$ • Cash Only
Most Photographed: Fried Dumplings
Don't Miss: Pan-fried pork & chive dumplings
$5 can get you so full here it's insane. skip the noodles, just get 4 orders of dumplings. the sesame pancake sandwich is also slept on.
insider tip: go at like 2pm on a wednesday. any other time the line is stupid long. also they dgaf about anything so don't expect service with a smile lol
Local Favorite

Abraço

★★★★★ (4.6/5)

Cozy espresso bar serving exceptional coffee and fresh pastries. The perfect morning stop in the East Village.

81 E 7th Street
$ • Morning Hours
Most Photographed: Latte Art
Don't Miss: The olive oil cake
abraço is tiny and always packed but the vibe is worth it. if you can grab a spot at the window bar, you're golden. best espresso in the neighborhood, no debate.
insider tip: get there right when they open at 8am or forget it. also they run out of pastries by like 10am so don't sleep.
Hidden Gem

Superiority Burger

★★★★★ (4.8/5)

Cult-favorite vegetarian counter serving creative burgers and sides. Minimalist space with maximum flavor.

119 Avenue A
$$ • Lunch & Dinner
Most Photographed: Superiority Burger
Don't Miss: The namesake Superiority Burger
not just for vegetarians trust me. the burnt broccoli salad will change your life. order at the counter, eat outside, watch the chaos of avenue a.
insider tip: they change the menu all the time so don't get attached to anything. also the gelato next door is run by the same people and it's incredible.
Historic Landmark

Katz's Delicatessen

★★★★☆ (4.4/5)

Legendary Jewish deli since 1888, famous for sky-high pastrami sandwiches and its role in "When Harry Met Sally."

205 E Houston Street
$$$ • Open Late
Most Photographed: Pastrami on Rye
Don't Miss: Pastrami on rye with a side of pickles
yeah it's touristy but it's touristy for a reason. go on a random tuesday at 11am and you'll be fine. the pastrami is actually that good. don't lose your ticket or they'll charge you $50.
insider tip: tip the slicer like $5 and they'll give you samples. also split a sandwich because they're massive. and grab extra pickles from the barrel.
